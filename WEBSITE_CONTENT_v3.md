## Strategic frame (not copy — internal reference)

The product has two distinct capability regimes:

**Market Intelligence** — external view only. Public data: reviews, competitor menus, pricing, market signals.
Useful, automated, runs on autopilot. Entry product or standalone for clients with capable internal teams.
Honest about what it is: the table stakes analytical layer. Not where the deepest insight lives.

**Full Intelligence** — integration-first. Disha connects to your actual business data (POS, reservations,
cost sheets, financials), runs agentic analysis against real numbers, and produces validated findings.
This is what a senior analytics professional does. This is the real product.

The public scan is one layer of Full Intelligence — not a separate product that happens to share a name.

---

### Track 1 — Market Intelligence

*For operators who want external market awareness on autopilot.*

**Step 01 — Submit your restaurant**
Enter the restaurant name and city. No setup, no integrations. Disha handles the research.

**Step 02 — Disha scans 8 public sources**
Google Reviews, TripAdvisor, TheFork, Wolt, your website, competitor menus, local market data,
and sector benchmarks — collected and structured automatically.

**Step 03 — Six specialist agents analyse**
Separate agents examine review patterns, competitor positioning, menu pricing, and market signals.
A synthesis agent connects their outputs into a coherent picture — every finding typed by
confidence: observed, estimated, or inferred.

**Step 04 — You receive a market intelligence report**
Where you stand relative to competitors. What your reviews are actually saying. Where your
pricing sits in the market. What to look at first.

---

### Track 2 — Full Intelligence

*For operators who want Disha to replace the analytics function they never had.*

**Step 01 — Integration setup (one-time)**
Disha maps your data: POS exports, reservation system, cost records, financial summaries.
A structured intake — no bespoke engineering required. Done once. Runs from there.

**Step 02 — Disha runs the full intelligence stack**
All 8 public sources plus your actual business data. Agents cross-reference your real margin
patterns against competitor pricing. Your POS trends against your review signals. Your cost
structure against market benchmarks. Estimated claims become validated findings.

**Step 03 — Validated intelligence report**
Not hypothesis — evidence. Your actual contribution margin by menu category. Revenue patterns
tied to specific operational signals. Financial estimates replaced by validated claims against
your own numbers.

**Step 04 — Ranked action plan with tracked experiments**
Every finding converts into a specific action ranked by expected impact — with the evidence,
the assumption, and the definition of success. Disha tracks whether you ran the experiments
and whether the outcomes moved.

## What's in the Report (Capabilities)

**Label:** What's in the report

**Headline:**
Six intelligence dimensions.
Deeper with every data source you add.

**Subheadline:**
Market Intelligence gives you the external view. Full Intelligence validates it against what's
actually happening in your business. Every capability improves when Disha has more to work with.

**6 capability cards (each with two-regime framing):**

---

**1. Review intelligence**
Icon: MessageSquare

Title: Review intelligence

Market Intelligence: Systematic analysis across all major platforms. Sentiment patterns, recurring
complaints, peak period issues — structured and ranked rather than read one at a time.

Full Intelligence: Review signals cross-referenced with your POS data. Cold food complaints on
weekend evenings correlate with specific prep patterns visible in your ticket times. The review
becomes the symptom; Disha finds the operational cause.

---

**2. Competitive intelligence**
Icon: Target

Title: Competitive intelligence

Market Intelligence: Your 8 nearest competitors mapped by cuisine, price tier, rating, and review
volume. Pricing gaps, positioning spaces, menu categories where you are under- or over-indexed.

Full Intelligence: Competitor positioning mapped against your actual margin data. Not just "you're
priced €3 below market" — but "you're priced €3 below market on dishes where your current food
cost makes that margin-negative at your volume."

---

**3. Menu and pricing analysis**
Icon: Receipt

Title: Menu and pricing analysis

Market Intelligence: Your price positioning relative to market averages for your cuisine and city.
Underpriced items identified against competitor menus. Menu engineering signals from review data.

Full Intelligence: Real contribution margin by dish, by category, by service period — calculated
against your actual cost data. Which items are driving revenue but destroying margin. Which are
priced below food cost. Menu engineering that normally takes a consultant two days, updated weekly.

---

**4. Financial signal intelligence**
Icon: TrendingUp

Title: Financial signal intelligence

Market Intelligence: Structural patterns surfaced from public signals — pricing relative to cost
benchmarks, discount behaviour, review-to-revenue correlations. Useful estimates. Clearly labelled
as estimates.

Full Intelligence: Estimates replaced by validated findings against your actual numbers. Margin
erosion traced to specific causes rather than hypothesised. Every financial claim shows its source,
its assumption, and its confidence level. This is what the €200,000 finding was built on.

---

**5. Ranked action plan**
Icon: ListOrdered

Title: Ranked action plan

Market Intelligence: Actions ranked by estimated impact, grounded in market signals. Each with
an effort estimate, time horizon, and evidence trail.

Full Intelligence: The same structure, but every rank is validated against your actual business
data. "Fix weekend delivery packaging" becomes "fix weekend delivery packaging — estimated €800–
1,400/month revenue recovery based on your actual Wolt revenue and the review-to-ranking
correlation in your specific market."

---

**6. 30-day experiments**
Icon: FlaskConical

Title: 30-day experiments

Market Intelligence: Low-risk, testable changes with clear hypotheses and success metrics.
Designed for in-house execution.

Full Intelligence: Experiments tied to specific metrics in your actual data. Before: "run a lunch
deal and see if covers improve." After: "run a lunch deal targeting the 11:30–13:00 window where
your POS data shows a 34% cover drop vs. the same period six months ago. Success metric: 15%
cover recovery within 30 days."
